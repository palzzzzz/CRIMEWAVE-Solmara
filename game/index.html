HOW TO PLAY

W A S DWalk / run (hold SHIFT). Mouse (click to capture) or drag to look; arrows also turn the camera
EEnter the nearest car / get out
W / S · A / DDriving: accelerate & brake / reverse, steer
SPACEHandbrake — slide round corners for drift cash
E on a moving carCarjack it — the driver is dragged out. Witnessed crimes raise your WANTED level; break line of sight and hide in the red search zone to shake the police
LEFT MOUSE · RIGHT MOUSEFire / throw / swing · hold to aim (camera goes over the shoulder). R reloads
HOLD Q · 1-6Weapon wheel (time slows) · or pick directly: fists, knife, pistol, SMG, shotgun, molotov
SPACE (on foot)Jump. Sprinting (SHIFT) drains stamina
HELICOPTERWalk to the pad in the harbour (marked H) and press E. SPACE up, SHIFT down, W/S, A/D, arrows strafe
PPhone: SolFeed (citizens post about your crimes, go viral and the heat climbs faster), messages and the mission log
ENTERAnswer a phone call / advance dialogue. Missions arrive as calls from Luna, Teo and Roxy
E at a shop counterWalk into the boutique, gun store or minimart on the boardwalk and buy. REP unlocks the big guns
G in a carIn Roxy's yard: repaint (clears stars), upgrades, store your personal car. In the harbour Fence yard: sell a stolen car
R in a carNext radio station (four stations, track titles on the HUD). M mutes
LCall or clear the rain
MMute sound
TABSwitch between Ray and Luna (on foot)
MOUSE WHEELCamera distance on foot
ESCRelease the mouse
